meet laundryman

He's serious. He's blue. He wears a laundry basket on his head and he will not tolerate a single unnecessary PASSED line in your Claude Code context.

The MCP server gives you true output replacement right now — Claude calls laundryman:run_tests and receives only the filtered result. No original noise. No compromise.

The PostToolUse hook fires automatically on every Bash call. Today it's additive (original still arrives alongside the filter). It becomes true replacement the moment Anthropic ships replaceToolOutput.
